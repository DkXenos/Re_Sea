Anything on a checkerboard needs a transparent background

The squares behind a picture are the page showing you there is nothing there. A JPEG has no transparency, so a JPEG of a bottle gives you a bottle on a white rectangle.

9‑slice files get stretched

Corners stay put, the middle stretches to fill a button or panel. A photograph smears. Use a plain frame or a flat texture with nothing important in the middle.

Size is a target, not a requirement

Drop in any resolution. The installer scales and centre‑crops to the size shown. Higher resolution is better; matching the shape matters more than matching the pixels.
